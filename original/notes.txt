Additional sound ideas:


1. "Pop Goes the Weasel"
Vibe: Playful, bouncy, and mischievous.

Why it works: It has a natural climax on the "Pop!" moment, which fits perfectly when starting a stage or clearing an objective.

Key Notes (C Major): C E F G | E C | G G F E | D C

2. "The Mexican Hat Dance" (Jarabe Tapatío)
Vibe: Festive, rapid-fire, and high energy.

Why it works: Shares a similar Latin rhythm and cultural nostalgia as "La Cucaracha". It’s instantly recognizable within just 4 or 5 notes.

Key Notes (C Major): G G C C | E E C C | G G C C | D E D C

3. "The Entertainer" (Scott Joplin)
Vibe: Whimsical, jaunty, and classic arcade.

Why it works: Ragtime syncopation translates remarkably well to 8-bit sound chips, giving the level a retro, lighthearted feel.

Key Notes (C Major): D D# E C E C E | C D D# E C D E | C

4. "Entry of the Gladiators" (Circus Theme)
Vibe: Chaotic, silly, and fast-paced.

Why it works: Perfect for a trickier or faster level where the player is dodging hazards in a frenzy.

Key Notes (Chromatic Run): A G# A B A G# A | F E F G F E F | D

5. "Yankee Doodle"
Vibe: Upbeat, marching tempo, straightforward.

Why it works: Extremely simple scalar motion that is almost impossible to mess up on simple sound hardware.

Key Notes (C Major): C C D E | C E D G | C C D E | C B G

6. "The Can-Can" (Orpheus in the Underworld)
Vibe: High-speed, repetitive, and frantic.

Why it works: Drives urgency. If a level requires quick movement or dodging fast bugs, this tempo gets the player moving.

Key Notes (C Major): C C C D E D C | D D D E F E D | E E E F G F E
